🐠 My Under the Sea Adventure!

Make your own ocean story. Choose a sea, add fish, make them swim and talk!

👀 LOOK → 🖱️ CLICK → 🧩 DRAG → 🟩 TEST

🚀 Open Scratch Editor ↗

Our mission: Make a fish swim through the ocean and meet another sea animal.

⏱️ Time: 45 minutes • Work from Step 1 to Step 7

0 of 7 steps finished

💡 Keep this page open in one tab. Open Scratch in another tab. Come back here when you need help.

1

🌊 Choose your ocean (5 min)

  1. Open Scratch using the blue button above.
  2. Click Choose a Backdrop 🖼️ at the bottom right.
  3. Search for Underwater.
  4. Click an underwater backdrop.

👀 You should see the sea behind the cat.

2

🐟 Add your fish (5 min)

  1. Click the cat sprite.
  2. Click the bin 🗑️ to delete the cat.
  3. Click Choose a Sprite 🐱 at the bottom right.
  4. Search for Fish. Click a fish.
  5. Drag your fish into the sea.
3

🏊 Make the fish swim (10 min)

  1. Click your fish sprite first.
  2. Click Code.
  3. From Events 🟨, drag the green flag block.
  4. From Control 🟧, add forever underneath.
  5. Inside forever, add the two Motion 🟦 blocks shown here.
  6. Click the green flag 🟩 to test.

🐠 Your fish should move and turn at the edge.

🏳️ when 🟩 clicked
↻ forever
move 5 steps
if on edge, bounce

If the fish swims upside down: click Direction below the stage and choose the left-right rotation style.

4

💬 Make your fish talk (5 min)

  1. Click your fish sprite.
  2. From Looks 🟪, find say Hello! for 2 seconds.
  3. Snap it just below the green flag block, above forever.
  4. Change the words to Hello! I am a fish!
  5. Click the green flag again.
🏳️ when 🟩 clicked
say Hello! I am a fish! for 2 seconds
↻ forever
move 5 steps
if on edge, bounce
5

🐙 Add a sea friend (7 min)

  1. Click Choose a Sprite 🐱 again.
  2. Search for Octopus, Crab or Shark.
  3. Choose one sea friend.
  4. Click the new sprite in the sprite list.
  5. Add the blocks shown here to the new sprite.
  6. Click the green flag. Watch both animals!
🏳️ when 🟩 clicked
say Hi, fish! for 2 seconds
6

✨ Make it your own (8 min)

Choose TWO challenges:

⭐ Easy: Change the fish speed from 5 to 10.

⭐ Easy: Change the fish's message.

⭐⭐ Try: Add another sea animal.

⭐⭐ Try: Make your sea friend move using the same swimming blocks.

🆘 Help! Something is not working

Nothing moves? Click the green flag and check that the blocks are joined together.

Wrong animal moves? Click the correct sprite at the bottom before adding blocks.

Fish disappears? Add “if on edge, bounce” inside “forever”.

Can't see the sea? Click Choose a Backdrop at the bottom right, not Choose a Sprite.

7

💾 Save and share (5 min)

  1. In Scratch, click File → Save to your computer to save your .sb3 project.
  2. If you are signed into Scratch, you can also name your project and save it online.
  3. Click File → Save to your computer before leaving.
  4. Upload your .sb3 file to Google Classroom as your work.

📸 If your teacher also wants a PDF, use Print → Save as PDF for this instruction sheet. The .sb3 file is the actual playable Scratch project.
